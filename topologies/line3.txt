# Three routers in a line, with an expensive direct link between the ends.
# Used by the live mode example in the README: the shortest path from 1 to 3
# runs through 2 at cost 20, not over the direct link at cost 50.
name line3
link 1 2 cost 10 delay 1000 bw 100000000
link 2 3 cost 10 delay 1000 bw 100000000
link 1 3 cost 50 delay 1000 bw 100000000
